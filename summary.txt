World news — Wednesday, 11 November 2026

Russia and Ukraine ended two days of US-mediated talks in Abu Dhabi agreeing their largest prisoner swap, over 1,000 a side, but no deal on territory or a ceasefire, Reuters reported.

Iran said a tanker caught fire after striking a mine in the Strait of Hormuz; the claim is unverified and no vessel was named. Britain's maritime agency also reported an incident there on Wednesday morning.

The Justice Department charged three Iran-backed men over nearly 20 alleged plots in the US and Europe, and Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent moved to a military base after Iranian death threats. British police also arrested a 41-year-old US Army officer with NATO's Allied Rapid Reaction Corps on suspicion of spying for Russia; he has not been charged.

Late on Tuesday, the House voted 211-204 to send articles impeaching Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth to committee, killing the effort; Republicans chose Jim Jordan as their next House leader over Steve Scalise, 106-73.

Israel swore in its new Knesset on Tuesday after a deadlocked election; Gadi Eisenkot, tasked with forming a government, has until 6 December.

At COP31 in Antalya, UN chief António Guterres warned the climate crisis is 'outrunning us'; the US stayed away for a second year, and Turkey pushed a goal of 35% of energy from electricity by 2035.

Confirmed Ebola cases in DR Congo passed 11,000, with 11,042 cases and 5,387 deaths, data showed on Tuesday.

October inflation came in hot at 0.4% on Tuesday, above the 0.3% forecast, lifting December Fed rate-rise odds as the 10-year yield briefly topped 5%. Stocks rallied on Wednesday — the S&P 500 up 1.3%, the Nasdaq 1.6%.

Anthropic set a $70-$78 IPO price range valuing it at up to $1.4 trillion; OpenAI's revenue rose about 50% to $10 billion in the quarter, the Wall Street Journal reported; Nvidia fell after hours on a report its board may oust Jensen Huang.

Delhi's air stayed poor after Diwali, at 299 on Wednesday morning.
