World News - Wednesday, 11 November 2026

Ukraine and Russia held a second day of US-mediated talks in Abu Dhabi and agreed a major prisoner swap - 1,000, possibly 1,500, people per side, the largest of the war - though territory remains unresolved.

In Britain, counterterrorism police arrested a 41-year-old US Army officer serving with a NATO unit on suspicion of spying for Russia. The US also charged three Iran-backed operatives over nearly 20 plots in Europe and America, and Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent was moved to a military base after threats.

In Washington, the House voted 211-204 to shelve impeachment articles against Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth, and Republicans chose Jim Jordan as House leader, beating Steve Scalise 106-73. October inflation came in hot at 0.4%, above the 0.3% forecast, lifting the odds of a December Fed rate rise; the 10-year Treasury yield touched 5.03%.

Markets fell on Tuesday - the Dow lost 0.3% to 52,232 and the S&P 500 0.4% - then steadied on Wednesday as CoreWeave's strong results lifted AI stocks. Anthropic filed for a Nasdaq listing that could value it at $1.4 trillion, while Nvidia faces a shareholder lawsuit over a Chinese spying scandal. OpenAI's revenue reached $10 billion in the third quarter. The NYSE picked Avalanche to settle tokenized stocks, and 21 banks committed $270 million to a stablecoin venture.

At COP31 in Antalya, 102 leaders gathered for a climate summit the United States is skipping for a second year. Tankers caught fire after explosions in the Strait of Hormuz. Israel swore in a new parliament with no government, and former Malaysian prime minister Najib Razak moved to house arrest after four years in prison over the 1MDB scandal. Delhi's air stayed poor, at an index of 299 after Diwali, and the Ebola outbreak in DR Congo neared 11,000 cases, with more than 5,300 deaths.
